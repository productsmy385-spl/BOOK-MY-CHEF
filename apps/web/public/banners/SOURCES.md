# Banner image sources

These are licensed stock photographs, **not AI-generated images**. Each was downloaded from
Unsplash and is covered by the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for
commercial use, no attribution required. Unsplash+ (paid) images were deliberately excluded.
Every image was reviewed before use: photos with readable brand text, logos or watermarks, or that
did not fit an Indian food marketplace, were rejected.

Files were cropped and compressed (desktop 1920×731, mobile 1080×1350, JPEG q78). They are seeded
as platform campaigns by migration `V9__seed_initial_banner_campaigns.sql` and can be replaced at
any time from Admin → Campaigns (upload, or generate with AI once `OPENAI_API_KEY` is set). Admin →
Images → "Copy to ImageKit" moves them to ImageKit once credentials are configured.
